# Workplace Health and Safety Policy

## 1. Objective
NovaCorp is committed to providing a safe, healthy, and hazard-free work environment for all employees, contractors, and visiting clients across our global facilities.

## 2. General Safety and Incident Reporting
- **Hazard Reporting**: Any safety hazard, equipment malfunction, liquid spill, or physical injury must be reported immediately to Facilities & Safety via extension 2222 or `safety@novacorp.local`.
- **First Aid Stations**: Fully stocked first aid kits, automated external defibrillators (AEDs), and eye wash stations are located on every floor next to the main elevators.
- **Certified First Responders**: Each office floor has designated, certified employee emergency wardens identified by orange high-visibility badges.

## 3. Emergency Evacuation and Fire Drills
- **Alarm Response**: When the fire or evacuation alarm sounds, all employees must immediately stop work, exit via the nearest stairwell, and proceed to the designated outdoor assembly point. Elevators must never be used during an alarm.
- **Mandatory Drills**: Mandatory building evacuation and earthquake safety drills are held semi-annually. All building occupants must participate.
- **Assembly Check-In**: Employees must report to their floor warden at the exterior assembly zone for headcount verification before re-entering the building.

## 4. Ergonomics and Workstation Setup
- **Ergonomic Assessments**: Employees experiencing physical discomfort or repetitive strain may request a formal ergonomic assessment through the Facilities Portal.
- **Adjustable Equipment**: NovaCorp provides sit-stand converters, ergonomic chairs, monitor arms, and specialized input peripherals upon recommendation by the safety coordinator.

## 5. Visitor Security and Access Control
- **Visitor Registration**: All visitors, vendors, and guests must sign in at the lobby reception, provide government-issued photo ID, and visibly wear a Visitor Badge at all times.
- **Escort Policy**: Unbadged or non-employee visitors must be escorted by an authorized NovaCorp staff member while on corporate premises. Tailgating through secure badge doors is strictly prohibited.
