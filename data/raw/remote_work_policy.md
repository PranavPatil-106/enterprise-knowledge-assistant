# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Purpose & Scope
NovaCorp Enterprises supports flexible working arrangements to foster employee work-life balance while ensuring business continuity and data security. This policy applies to all eligible salaried employees.

## 2. Work Models
- **Fully Remote**: Employees residing more than 50 miles from a regional office may work remotely full-time upon director-level approval.
- **Hybrid Model**: Standard employees are expected to work 2 designated days in the office and up to 3 days remotely per week.
- **On-site Core Days**: Tuesdays and Thursdays are designated as core collaboration days for all hybrid teams.

## 3. Core Working Hours
All remote and hybrid employees must be reachable and available for synchronous communication during core business hours:
- **Core Hours**: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in the employee's designated primary time zone.
- **Status Updates**: Employees are required to maintain active status indicators on Slack/Teams during working hours.

## 4. Home Office Stipend and Equipment
- **Hardware Provided**: NovaCorp provides a standard laptop, external monitor, secure headset, and docking station.
- **Internet Subsidy**: A monthly internet reimbursement of $50 is available upon submitting an expense report.
- **Ergonomic Allowance**: A one-time setup stipend of $400 is provided to new remote hires for ergonomic desk furniture.

## 5. Security and Environment Requirements
- Remote workspaces must be private and secure during customer or confidential data discussions.
- All enterprise systems must be accessed strictly through the corporate VPN with multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Public Wi-Fi networks (e.g., cafes, airports) cannot be used without activating the corporate VPN.
