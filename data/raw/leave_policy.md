# Enterprise Leave Policy

## 1. Overview and Purpose
This document outlines the standard leave entitlements, eligibility criteria, and request procedures for full-time and part-time employees at NovaCorp Enterprises.

## 2. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
- **Accrual Rate**: Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of PTO per calendar month worked (equivalent to 20 days per year).
- **Rollover Limit**: Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Unused days beyond 5 will lapse on December 31st.
- **Notice Period**: Planned PTO requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days must be submitted via the HR Portal at least 2 weeks in advance.

## 3. Sick and Medical Leave
- **Entitlement**: Employees are provided 10 days of paid sick leave annually, credited on January 1st.
- **Medical Certification**: A medical certificate from a licensed physician is required for sick leave absences exceeding 3 consecutive working days.
- **Emergency Notification**: Employees must inform their direct manager by 9:00 AM on the day of unplanned absence.

## 4. Parental Leave
- **Primary Caregivers**: Eligible for up to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- **Secondary Caregivers**: Eligible for up to 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
- **Eligibility**: Employees must have completed at least 6 continuous months of service prior to the expected date of leave.

## 5. Bereavement Leave
- Up to 5 consecutive paid business days for immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- Up to 3 consecutive paid business days for extended family members (grandparents, in-laws).

## 6. Request and Approval Process
All leave requests must be logged through the internal HR Portal (`portal.novacorp.local/leave`). Approvals are processed by the employee's line manager within 48 business hours.
