# Comprehensive Employee Benefits Policy

## 1. Overview
NovaCorp India offers a comprehensive total rewards package designed to support the physical health, financial well-being, and continuous professional growth of our employees and their families.

## 2. Healthcare and Insurance Coverage
- **Group Medical and Dental Plans**: Full-time employees and their dependents are covered under the corporate Group Mediclaim Insurance (GMC) and Group Personal Accident (GPA) policies, effective from day one of joining.
- **Employer Contribution**: NovaCorp fully sponsors 100% of the premium for employees and subsidizes 80% of the premium for spouse and children.
- **Term Life Insurance**: The company provides group term life insurance coverage equal to 3x annual Cost to Company (CTC) at no cost to the employee.

## 3. Retirement, Provident Fund (EPF), and Gratuity
- **Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)**: In accordance with the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, the company contributes 12% of basic salary towards the Employee Provident Fund (EPF) and Employee Pension Scheme (EPS), matching the employee's mandatory 12% contribution.
- **Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF)**: Employees may opt to contribute an additional percentage of their basic salary towards VPF through payroll deductions.
- **National Pension System (NPS)**: Employees can enroll in the Corporate NPS scheme under Section 80CCD(2), with an employer contribution of up to 10% of basic salary for retirement savings and tax optimization.
- **Gratuity**: Gratuity benefits are payable in accordance with the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 upon separation after completing at least 5 continuous years of service.

## 4. Wellness and Fitness Stipend
- **Monthly Allowance**: Employees may claim up to ₹5,000 per month (₹60,000 annually) for gym memberships, fitness studio classes, home exercise equipment, or mental wellness subscriptions.
- **Reimbursement Procedure**: Claims must be submitted through the Benefits Portal under the "Wellness Program" category with GST-compliant payment receipts.

## 5. Tuition and Continuing Education Reimbursement
- **Annual Limit**: Full-time employees with over 12 months of tenure may receive up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year for accredited degree courses, certifications, or professional executive programs relevant to their role.
- **Grade Requirement**: Reimbursement requires a minimum grade of "B" or "Pass" for graded academic courses.
