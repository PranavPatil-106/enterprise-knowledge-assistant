# Business Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines reimbursement standards, allowable business travel expenses, and payment procedures for all NovaCorp employees conducting authorized company business.

## 2. Travel Booking and Airfare
- **Advance Booking**: Domestic flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance; international flights require at least 21 days advance notice.
- **Flight Class**: Economy class is standard for domestic flights under 6 continuous hours. Premium Economy or Business Class requires Vice President approval for flights exceeding 6 continuous hours.
- **Preferred Booking Portal**: All flights, trains, and rental vehicles must be reserved through the NovaCorp Travel Portal (`travel.novacorp.local`).

## 3. Lodging and Accommodation
- **Nightly Room Rate**: The standard maximum lodging rate is $220 per night for tier-1 cities (e.g., New York, San Francisco, London) and $160 per night for other locations, excluding taxes.
- **Room Incidentals**: Personal mini-bar expenses, hotel spa services, and in-room pay-per-view entertainment are non-reimbursable.

## 4. Daily Meals and Per Diem
- **Per Diem Rate**: Employees receive a daily meal allowance of up to $75 per day during active travel ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner).
- **Alcohol**: Alcoholic beverages are reimbursable only when entertaining prospective clients with prior managerial pre-authorization.
- **Itemized Receipts**: An itemized receipt showing individual items purchased is required for any meal expense exceeding $25.

## 5. Ground Transportation and Mileage
- **Rideshare and Taxis**: Standard Uber, Lyft, or licensed taxi services are reimbursable between airport, hotel, and client work sites. Luxury vehicles (Uber Black) are not eligible for reimbursement.
- **Personal Vehicle Mileage**: Employees using personal vehicles for business travel are reimbursed at the standard rate of $0.67 per mile.

## 6. Expense Submission and Reimbursement
- **Submission Timeline**: All expense claims and itemized receipts must be submitted through the Finance Expense Portal within 30 days of travel completion.
- **Approval and Payout**: Validated expense reports are approved by the Department Head and paid out on the bi-weekly payroll cycle.
