# Code of Conduct and Ethics Policy

## 1. Overview
NovaCorp is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of professional ethics, integrity, and lawful conduct across all global operations, business partnerships, and workplace interactions.

## 2. Workplace Respect and Anti-Harassment
- **Zero Tolerance**: Harassment, bullying, or discrimination based on race, gender, religion, age, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin is strictly prohibited.
- **Reporting Channels**: Employees experiencing or witnessing inappropriate conduct should report incidents to their supervisor, People Operations, or via the anonymous Ethics Hotline at `ethics@novacorp.local`.
- **Non-Retaliation Guarantee**: NovaCorp guarantees strict protection against retaliation for any employee who reports concerns or participates in investigations in good faith.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest
- **Outside Employment**: Full-time employees must receive prior written consent from Human Resources before taking on outside consulting or secondary employment.
- **Personal Relationships**: Consensual romantic relationships between managers and direct reports must be disclosed to ensure appropriate reporting realignment and prevent favoritism.
- **Financial Interests**: Employees may not hold significant financial interests in competitors, suppliers, or client organizations without Executive Committee disclosure.

## 4. Gifts, Meals, and Entertainment
- **Gift Threshold**: Employees may accept nominal promotional gifts up to a maximum value of $50 per calendar year from suppliers or clients.
- **Prohibited Items**: Accepting cash, gift cards, luxury items, or paid personal vacations from any vendor or client is strictly forbidden.
- **Bribery and Anti-Corruption**: Any attempt to offer, promise, or provide improper payments to domestic or foreign government officials violates federal law and will result in immediate termination.

## 5. Whistleblower Protection and Investigation
- All ethics complaints are logged and investigated within 5 business days by an independent review team.
- The Ethics Hotline is available 24/7/365 at toll-free ext. 8888.
